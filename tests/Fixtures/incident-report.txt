Incident report: checkout outage, 14 March

Between 09:12 and 10:05 the checkout page returned errors
for about 40% of customers. The cause was a database
migration that added an index to the orders table without
the CONCURRENTLY option, which locked the table.

Around 310 orders failed during the outage. Support
received 57 tickets. Engineering rolled the migration
back at 10:05 and service recovered within two minutes.

We do not yet know whether any customers were charged
without an order being created. Finance is checking the
payment provider's records and expects an answer by
Friday.

Follow-up: migrations that touch large tables must be
reviewed by the database team before release. Priya
will add a check to the deployment pipeline next sprint.
