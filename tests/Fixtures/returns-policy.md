# Acme returns policy

You can return most items within 30 days of delivery for a
full refund. Items must be unused and in their original
packaging.

Opened electronics can be returned within 14 days and are
refunded minus a 10% restocking fee.

If an item arrives damaged, tell us within 48 hours. We pay
the return shipping and send a replacement or a refund.

Personalised items and gift cards cannot be returned.

Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7
working days of the return reaching our warehouse.
