# Shipping

Orders placed before 14:00 IST on a working day ship the same
day. Standard delivery takes 2 to 5 working days in India and
is free above INR 1,000; below that it costs INR 79.

Express delivery (1 to 2 working days) costs INR 249 and is
available in metro cities only.

We ship with BlueDart and Delhivery. A tracking number is
emailed when the parcel leaves our warehouse.

We do not ship outside India.
