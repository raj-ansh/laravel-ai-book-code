# Brightlite Retail: trade terms with Acme

These terms apply to Brightlite Retail's trade account only.

## Payment

Brightlite pays approved invoices within 45 days of delivery.
Orders above INR 5,00,000 receive a trade discount of 8%.

## Account manager

Brightlite's account manager at Acme is Kavya Iyer. Kavya
handles quotes, bulk orders and delivery schedules for all
Brightlite stores.

## Deliveries

Bulk deliveries go to Brightlite's Pune warehouse on Tuesdays
and Fridays. Damaged items in a bulk delivery must be reported
within 7 days of delivery, with photos.
