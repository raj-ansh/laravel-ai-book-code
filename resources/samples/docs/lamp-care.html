Lamp care guide

Switch off and unplug a lamp before cleaning it. Let bulbs cool for ten minutes.

Brass

Wipe brass with a soft dry cloth. Do not use metal polish on lacquered brass: it removes the lacquer and the brass will tarnish.

Fabric shades

Remove dust with a lint roller or a soft brush. Do not wash linen shades; spot-clean with a damp cloth.

Bulbs

Use only LED bulbs of 9 watts or less in lamps with fabric shades.
